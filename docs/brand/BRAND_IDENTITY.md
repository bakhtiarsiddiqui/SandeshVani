# SandeshVani — Brand Identity and Domain Review

**Version:** 2.0 planning note  
**Review date:** 2026-11-04  
**Status:** Working brand selected; domain selected by project owner; registration and legal clearance pending

## Brand decision

- **Name:** SandeshVani
- **Hindi script:** संदेशवाणी
- **Name construction:** Sandesh (संदेश — message) + Vani (वाणी — voice, expression or communication)
- **Meaning / interpretation:** a voice that carries important messages to society
- **Category:** Civic Communication Technology / public-awareness platform
- **Primary audience:** public departments, universities, NGOs and other institutions
- **Brand attributes:** trustworthy, inclusive, clear, accessible, dependable and modern
- **Primary tagline:** “Har zaroori baat, har zaroori insaan tak.”
- **English descriptor:** Public communication and awareness infrastructure
- **Selected domain:** `sandeshvani.in`

## Brand story

Every important message deserves to be heard. SandeshVani exists to bridge the gap between institutions and communities. Through technology, multilingual communication and accessible public-awareness campaigns, it helps important information reach the people who need it. Its vision is to build a more informed, connected and aware society.

The product must earn trust through accessible design, consent-aware audience management, accountable approvals, tenant isolation and reliable operations—not through branding alone.

## Product naming direction

Use **SandeshVani** as the public-facing product brand. Keep module names descriptive until validated with users:

- SandeshVani Campaigns — campaign authoring and approvals
- SandeshVani Connect — audiences and communication preferences
- SandeshVani Bhasha — language variants and translation workflows
- SandeshVani Intelligence — AI-assisted content and language tools; human review remains mandatory
- SandeshVani Insights — reporting and analytics
- SandeshVani Samvaad — future feedback and two-way engagement

These are working labels, not separately cleared product marks. Avoid presenting AI assistance as autonomous approval or dispatch.

| Social handles | **OPEN** | Check platform availability and impersonation/conflict risk. |

The earlier name **Sandesh (संदेश)** was not retained as the product brand. Prior research noted that `sandesh.com` is registered and associated with The Sandesh Limited; this is a reason to avoid assuming that a similar name is legally clear. SandeshVani is a distinct selected working name, but still requires independent trademark and market-conflict review.

## Required before public launch

1. Register `sandeshvani.in` through an accredited registrar if it remains available; verify successful registration in the registrar account and registry records.
2. Search the IP India Trade Marks Registry for **SandeshVani**, phonetic and visual variants, and relevant software, communication, SaaS and public-service categories; have counsel assess results.
3. Review existing companies, apps, media products and social handles using the same or confusingly similar name.
4. Validate pronunciation, spelling and cultural interpretation with Hindi speakers and intended user communities.
5. Configure MFA, registrar lock, auto-renewal, recovery contacts and DNS security after registration.
